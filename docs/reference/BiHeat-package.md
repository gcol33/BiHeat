# BiHeat: Diagonally Split Heatmaps for Paired Matrix Visualization

Visualizes two numeric matrices of identical shape in a single heatmap
by splitting each cell along a diagonal. The upper triangle encodes one
matrix (default white to red) and the lower triangle encodes the other
(default white to blue). Useful for compact paired comparisons such as
observed vs. predicted, mean vs. variance, correlation vs. significance,
or train vs. test performance. Built on 'ggplot2'.
